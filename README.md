# Dayeon Yoo

Master’s student in Computer Engineering at the University of New Mexico, advised by Prof. Milad Marvian.

I work on computational methods for quantum error correction, with a focus on automated qLDPC code discovery and optimization, including hypergraph product and bivariate bicycle codes.

My broader interests include:

- Quantum LDPC codes
- BP+OSD and neural decoding
- Logical computation
- Hardware-aware quantum error correction
- Scientific computing and HPC

## Current research

**Automated Discovery of Hypergraph Product and Bivariate Bicycle Codes via Suppression of Low-Weight Logical Patterns**

Developing search objectives based on low-weight logical patterns to identify improved qLDPC code constructions without repeatedly running logical-error-rate simulations.
